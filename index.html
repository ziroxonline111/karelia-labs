Built to stay inside your platform

The Atlassian apps are Forge apps. They make no calls to outside servers, so your data stays in Atlassian.

Karelia Translate runs on your own Flarum server and talks only to the translation service you choose. None of our apps sends your data to us.

Read the security overview

Storage
Atlassian apps: your Confluence or Jira site and Forge storage on it. Flarum: your own database
Network
Atlassian apps: no calls to outside servers. Flarum: only the translation service you choose
Permissions
Granular scopes or Flarum permissions. Actions run as the person using the app
