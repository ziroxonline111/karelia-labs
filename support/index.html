Support for each app

Every app has its own support page with what to include in a request and answers to common questions.

  • Treewright: Page tree copier and templates for Confluence
  • Specfold: OpenAPI and AsyncAPI viewer for Confluence
  • Q&A Knowledge Base: Questions and answers for Confluence
  • Word and PDF Document Generator: Word, PDF and Excel documents from Jira issues
  • Karelia Translate: AI translation of posts for Flarum 2 forums

Response times

For the Atlassian apps:

  • Issues that Atlassian classifies as critical: we aim to reply within 24 hours.
  • Other requests: we aim to reply within 5 business days.

These times apply during Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 Central European Time.

For Karelia Translate:

  • Errors that stop translations from working: we aim to reply within 2 business days.
  • Other requests: we aim to reply within 5 business days.

What we help with

We help with installing and configuring our apps, with errors and with behaviour you did not expect. We explain how a feature works and what its limits are.

For the Word and PDF Document Generator, support for the content and layout of your own templates is limited to explaining how its tags and filters work.

Billing, licenses and subscriptions for apps bought through the Atlassian Marketplace are handled by Atlassian. Subscriptions, trials, invoices and refunds for Karelia Translate are handled by Floxum. Support for the apps themselves comes from Karelia Labs.

Security issues

Please do not report a vulnerability through public channels. Write to support@karelialabs.atlassian.net. The security page says what to include.
