Security and data

Karelia Tickets runs on your own Statamic server. Payments are taken by Stripe in your own account, orders stay on your server, and it never contacts us.

Your Stripe account, your buyers

Payments go straight to your own Stripe account, card details never touch your site, and orders stay on your server. The addon takes no fee per ticket and never contacts us.

Where your data goes

On your own server, which talks over HTTPS to api.stripe.com with your Stripe key to open Checkout, read back a payment and make refunds, and receives Stripe's signed webhooks. Buyers pay on Stripe's own checkout page. Confirmation emails go through the mail service you configure in Laravel. The addon never contacts Karelia Labs: it has no telemetry, no analytics and no account with us. Statamic itself checks licence keys with statamic.com; that is part of Statamic, not of the addon.

What is stored

Everything the addon stores is in one folder, storage/statamic/karelia-tickets, outside the public web folder.

DataWhere and whyHow long
Orders: the buyer's name and email, the event, ticket types, quantities, prices and statusOne JSON file per order. Issue tickets, refunds and attendee listsUntil you delete the order
Tickets: codes, the name on each, and when and by whom each was checked inThe order file. Let each ticket in onceAs above
Stripe references: Checkout Session, PaymentIntent and refund IDs. Never card detailsThe order file. Match and refund paymentsAs above
The time and type of the last webhook from StripeLaravel's cache. Show in the Control Panel that the webhook worksUntil the cache is cleared
Settings: collections, currency, ticket note, a copy-to addressStatamic's addon settings. Stripe keys only in .envUntil changed

Personal data

Orders hold the buyer's name and email address, and the names of staff who checked tickets in. Stripe receives the buyer's email, the ticket names and prices and the order number. Your site, as the event organiser, is the controller of this data. To erase a buyer's data, delete their order file.

Permissions

The addon adds three Statamic permissions in a Tickets group under Users, Roles.

PermissionWhy the app needs it
View tickets and ordersSales, orders and attendee lists, including buyers' names and emails
Refund, cancel and resend orders, and add free ticketsChange orders. Needs the permission above
Check in attendeesThe check-in page only. Shows the name on each scanned ticket

Access control

Every Control Panel request checks these permissions on the server. Ticket pages for buyers are reached only with a 40-character random link sent to them, are marked noindex and are never cached. The Stripe webhook accepts only events with a valid signature from your endpoint secret, at most five minutes old.

Untrusted input

Ticket orders are validated on the server: the event must be on sale, the ticket type must exist and be on sale, quantities are limited by what is left and the most per order, and the checkout is rate-limited with a hidden spam trap. Prices always come from the event, never from the browser. The attendee CSV is written so spreadsheet programs do not run cells as formulas.

Dependencies

The server side depends on Statamic, Laravel and chillerlan/php-qrcode (MIT or Apache-2.0) for the QR codes, installed by Composer. Stripe is called with Laravel's HTTP client, without Stripe's SDK. The check-in page bundles qr-scanner (MIT), which reads the camera image in the browser; the image never leaves the device.

Other points

Overselling

Tickets are reserved under a lock per event, so two buyers cannot take the last seat. On several servers, use a shared cache store such as Redis so the lock is shared too.

Payments that arrive late

A buyer who pays after their hold ran out still gets the tickets they paid for, and the order is marked so you can check the capacity.

Logging

The addon writes nothing to logs during normal use. A failed email or Stripe call is reported to Laravel's log as any error would be, and a failed email is also shown on the order.

Sub-processors

None for us: we receive no site data. Stripe processes payments under your own agreement with Stripe. Licences and payments for the addon are handled by Statamic and Stripe. For the other parties involved in our own support and bookkeeping, see the privacy policy.

AI and language models

None. The addon contains no language model and sends no content to any.

Vulnerabilities

Report a suspected vulnerability to support@karelialabs.atlassian.net. Please include what you found, how to reproduce it and which version is affected, and give us a reasonable time to fix it before you publish details. We read every report.

Deleting data

Orders are kept until you delete them: delete an order's file to remove it, or the folder to remove all. Payment records in Stripe are kept by Stripe. Removing the addon with Composer leaves the orders folder in place.
