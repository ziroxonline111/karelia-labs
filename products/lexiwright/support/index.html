Response times

  • Issues that Atlassian classifies as critical: we aim to reply within 24 hours.
  • Other requests: we aim to reply within 5 business days.

These times apply during Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 Central European Time.

What to include

A good request lets us answer without asking questions first.

  • The address of your Confluence site and the page involved.
  • Where it happened: the glossary, the page byline, Link glossary terms, a macro, the settings or the import.
  • For import problems: the line numbers from the preview. Please do not send your whole glossary unless we ask for it.
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead. A screenshot helps.
  • When it happened, with your time zone.

Please do not send passwords or confidential content. If we need an example, we will ask for it.

Common questions

The byline says Glossary without a number.

No approved or deprecated glossary term was found in the text of the page. Terms are matched as whole words, with their synonyms, abbreviations and plurals. Suggested terms are not counted until an editor approves them.

Link glossary terms finds nothing to link.

Every term found in the page is linked already, or no term occurs in plain body text. Headings, links, code and other macros are left alone, and suggested terms are not linked until an editor approves them.

I am an administrator but can only suggest terms.

When editing is limited to groups, Confluence administrators are recognised by membership of one of the groups site-admins, confluence-admins, confluence-administrators, administrators or org-admins. Add yourself to one of the editor groups, or ask another administrator to change the setting.

A linked word lost its bold formatting.

A glossary link cannot carry formatting, so a term that is bold in the text loses its bold when it is linked. The text around it keeps its formatting, and the page history keeps the version from before the linking.

Why are terms not highlighted all over the page?

Forge apps cannot change how Confluence draws a page. The byline lists the terms of every page, and Link glossary terms turns the first occurrence of each into a link to its definition.

How do I export the glossary?

Open the settings page Lexiwright glossary, choose Export and then Create the export. Select all the text, copy it and save it as a .csv file. It uses the same columns as the import.

The glossary is not available.

Lexiwright needs an active licence. A site administrator can start a free trial or subscribe in the Atlassian Marketplace. The terms stay stored while the licence is not active.

Security issues

If you believe you have found a vulnerability, do not post it publicly. Write to support@karelialabs.com instead. The security page says what to include.
