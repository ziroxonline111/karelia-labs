2.3 The Customer may change the source code for use on its licensed Sites.

2.4 The Addon is licensed, not sold. The Provider keeps all rights in the Addon not expressly granted. Third-party components keep their own licences.

The price is shown on the Addon's page on the Statamic Marketplace. Checkout, taxes, receipts, licence keys, renewals and refunds are handled by Statamic under its terms for the Marketplace. The Provider may change prices for future purchases and renewals.

5. Updates and renewal

5.1 A Licence includes every new version released during the Update period. After the Update period the Addon keeps working on the version the Customer has, without time limit. A renewal through the Statamic Marketplace starts a new Update period.

5.2 The Provider aims to support each new major version of Statamic within four weeks of its release. Updates may change or remove features; the Provider will not materially reduce the core functionality of a version the Customer has paid for.

6. Support

The Provider provides support through the support portal or by email at support@karelialabs.atlassian.net, in English. The Provider aims to answer requests about errors that stop the Addon from working within 2 business days and other requests within 5 business days, during Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 Central European Time.

7. Customer data and privacy

7.1 The Addon runs on the Customer's own server. The Provider does not receive or have access to the Customer's content, files or users. How the Addon handles personal data is described in the Privacy Policy (https://ziroxonline111.github.io/karelia-labs/products/karelia-trash/privacy/), which forms part of this Agreement.

7.2 The Addon keeps copies of deleted items, which may include personal data in form submissions and, if the Customer switches it on, user accounts. The Customer is the controller of that data and is responsible for choosing a retention period and settings that match its own privacy policy and legal obligations.

8. No replacement for backups

The trash keeps a copy of items deleted through Statamic. It does not capture files removed outside Statamic, it is not a revision history and it is not a backup. The Customer should keep regular backups of its Site.
