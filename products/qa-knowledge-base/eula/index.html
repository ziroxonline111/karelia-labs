1. Definitions

  • Atlassian Product: the Atlassian Confluence Cloud site on which the App is installed.
  • Authorised Users: the users of the Customer's Atlassian Product, up to the number covered by the Customer's subscription tier.
  • Marketplace: the Atlassian Marketplace, through which the App is licensed and paid for.
  • Subscription: the Customer's licence for the App, bought or trialled via the Marketplace.
  • Q&A Content: the questions, answers, comments, tags and votes that Authorised Users create with the App, and data the Customer imports into it.

2.2 The number of Authorised Users follows the user tier of the Subscription.

2.3 The App is licensed, not sold. The Provider keeps all rights in the App not expressly granted.

The Customer shall not (a) copy, modify, reverse engineer or decompile the App except as law allows; (b) circumvent the licence checks; (c) provide the App to third parties as a service; (d) use the App unlawfully or to infringe others' rights; (e) use the App on a site where it is not licensed.

4. Fees, taxes and payment

Fees are set out in the Marketplace listing and are charged and collected by Atlassian under the Marketplace terms. Taxes, refunds, invoices and renewal are governed by the Atlassian Marketplace terms and the Customer's agreement with Atlassian. The Provider may change prices for future periods as the Marketplace allows. A tier that the listing offers free of charge is licensed under this Agreement without a fee.

5. Trial and evaluation

A trial licence gives full functionality for the trial period shown in the Marketplace. After it ends the App stops working for that site until a Subscription is bought.

6. Support and updates

6.1 The Provider provides support through the support page or by email at support@karelialabs.atlassian.net. The Provider aims to answer issues that Atlassian classifies as critical within 24 hours and other issues within 5 business days, during Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 Central European Time.

6.2 The Provider may release updates through the Marketplace. Updates may change or remove features; the Provider will not materially reduce the core functionality during a paid term without notice.

7. Customer data, Q&A Content and privacy

7.1 The App runs on the Atlassian Forge platform. The Provider does not receive or have access to the Customer's content or the data the App stores on the Customer's site. How the App handles personal data is described in the Privacy Policy (https://ziroxonline111.github.io/karelia-labs/products/qa-knowledge-base/privacy/), which forms part of this Agreement.

7.2 The Q&A Content belongs to the Customer. The Customer is responsible for it, for the conduct of its users, for moderating it, for having the right to import any data it imports, and for deciding which groups may ask, answer and moderate. The App acts with the permissions of the user who performs an action; except as described in the Privacy Policy it cannot access more than that user can.

7.3 Questions, answers and comments are stored by Confluence under the Customer's agreement with Atlassian. The remaining data of the App is stored in Forge storage on the Customer's site. The Customer can delete Q&A Content in the App and can export it as a CSV file. Uninstalling the App removes the App's own stored data according to the rules of the Atlassian Forge platform; the Provider does not control what the platform keeps for Confluence content.

7.4 Optional Rovo features (an action and an agent) are provided on Atlassian's Rovo platform; Atlassian's terms apply to what Rovo does with the information the App returns to it.

The Provider owns the App and its documentation. The Customer owns its Q&A Content. The Customer grants the Provider a free, perpetual licence to use feedback and suggestions about the App without obligation.

9. Third-party components

The App uses the Atlassian Forge platform and open source components under their own licences. The Atlassian Product and Forge are governed by the Customer's agreements with Atlassian.

10. Warranty disclaimer

To the extent permitted by law the App is provided "as is" and "as available". The Provider gives no warranty of merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose, non-infringement, uninterrupted or error-free operation, that search results are complete, that answers in the App are correct, or that an import or export is complete or identical to the source in every case. The Customer should check the result of an import, keep backups of important content, and not rely on answers in the App without checking them where it matters.

11.1 To the extent permitted by law the Provider is not liable for indirect, incidental, special or consequential damage, loss of profit, revenue, data or goodwill.

11.2 The Provider's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees the Customer paid for the App in the twelve months before the event giving rise to the claim.

12.1 This Agreement runs while the App is installed or the Subscription is active.

12.2 Either party may terminate for the other's material breach that is not remedied within 30 days of notice. The Provider may suspend the licence if the Subscription is not valid. On termination the Customer must stop using the App and uninstall it.

12.3 Sections 3, 8 and 10 to 14 survive termination.

The Provider may update this Agreement for future use by publishing a new version and informing Customers through the Marketplace or the App. Continued use after the effective date means acceptance.
