What it does

A handbook, a report or a runbook that leaves the company should carry the company's own look: the logo, the header and footer, the cover page, the heading styles. Make a Word template once, upload it, and Brandwright fills it with the content of a page or of a whole page tree.

Export from the page menu as Word, PDF or both, download the files or attach them to the page. Pages are read as the person who exports, made into documents inside Atlassian Forge, and nothing is sent to an outside conversion service.

  • Your own Word template

    Design it in Word once and add plain tags such as {title} and {@body} where the facts and the content go. Tags work in the body, header and footer, and a table of contents field is filled with the headings.

  • A page or a whole tree

    Export one page, or a page and its child pages in tree order (up to 100), as Word, PDF or both. Exports run in the background with progress, and the files can be attached to the page.

  • Page content that stays page content

    Headings, lists, tables with merged cells, code blocks, panels, task lists, status lozenges, dates, mentions, emoji and PNG or JPEG pictures are carried over. What cannot be carried over is listed.

  • Starter templates and a library

    Three starter templates, and a settings page to upload templates, see which tags each one uses, set the default and delete templates. A broken tag is caught at upload.

  • Respects permissions

    The export runs as the person who starts it, so pages that person cannot see are never exported.

  • Safe with files you did not write

    Macro-enabled files, old .doc files and password-protected files are refused, and no code from a template is ever run.

Built only on Atlassian Forge

Brandwright is built only on Atlassian Forge. Its manifest allows no outbound network access and has no remotes, no web triggers and no Connect modules. It makes no calls to outside servers, and your data stays in Atlassian: in Confluence itself and in Forge storage on your site. Its eligibility for Atlassian's Runs on Atlassian program has not been confirmed yet.

Good to know

  • The PDF is a clean rendition of the Word file, not a pixel-perfect copy. Text boxes, shapes, footnotes and columns of a template stay in the Word file only.
  • In the PDF the table of contents has no page numbers. In the Word file, answer Yes when Word asks to update fields, and the page numbers appear.
  • Pages are exported; blog posts are not. A tree is up to 100 pages.
  • Pictures are PNG and JPEG attachments of the page, up to 60 per document and 5 MB each. Macros such as the contents macro are not exported, and the dialog lists them.
  • A template can be up to 10 MB. Support for your own templates is limited to explaining how the tags work.
  • Microsoft Word is a trademark of the Microsoft group of companies. Brandwright does not use Microsoft software and is not affiliated with Microsoft.

Data and security

Brandwright runs entirely on Atlassian. Pages are read through the Confluence API as the person who exports, made into documents inside Forge, and kept for download for three days. It makes no calls to outside servers. The security page lists what is stored, for how long and which permissions are needed. The privacy policy and the EULA are public.
