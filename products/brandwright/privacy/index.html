1. What this policy covers

Brandwright ("the App") is an app for Atlassian Confluence Cloud built on the Atlassian Forge platform. It exports Confluence pages and page trees to Word and PDF files through Word templates that the customer uploads. This policy explains what data the App handles and what we, the provider, do and do not receive.

2. The short version

  • The App runs entirely on Atlassian's infrastructure (Atlassian Forge). It makes no calls to outside servers and does not send customer data to us or to any third party.
  • The App stores data in Atlassian's Forge hosted storage on the customer's behalf: templates, and for a limited time export jobs and the exported files (section 3). We cannot read this data.
  • We do not receive page content, templates, documents, names or email addresses from the App.
  • The App contains no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no cookies of its own, and loads no external scripts, fonts or images.
  • Stored personal data is limited to Atlassian account ids. The App takes part in Atlassian's personal data reporting.

4. Data we receive as the provider

  • Support requests: if you contact support we receive what you send us (typically name, email address, site URL and the problem description). We use it to answer you and keep it for at most 24 months after the last message, unless a longer period is required by law (for example bookkeeping rules). Please do not send us page content or templates unless we ask for an example.
  • Marketplace transactions: purchases, licences and billing are handled by Atlassian under the Atlassian Marketplace terms. We receive licence and sales information from Atlassian as described in the Marketplace Partner Agreement, such as site, tier and contact details of the technical and billing contacts.
  • Website: the site that publishes this policy sets no cookies and uses no analytics or trackers.

5. Roles and legal basis

For data handled by the App on the customer's site, the customer decides why and how it is used and is the controller. Atlassian hosts and processes it as the customer's processor under Atlassian's terms and the Forge Data Processing Addendum. The App's code runs on that platform and stores its data there; we have no access to the stored data, so we do not process the customer's personal data on our servers. For the support requests you send us, we are the controller of that correspondence.

For our own data (support, partner and billing contacts) we are the controller. The legal basis is our contract with you, our legitimate interest in running and supporting the App, and legal obligations (bookkeeping).

6. Sub-processors and recipients

We use no sub-processors for customer data. Atlassian (platform, hosting, billing, Marketplace) acts under its own terms. Support requests, whether sent through our support portal or by email, are handled in Atlassian's Jira Service Management, with Atlassian as our processor under its Data Processing Addendum.

7. International transfers

Customer data stays where the customer's Atlassian product keeps it; the Forge functions and the Forge hosted storage of the App follow the data residency of the host product. Our own support data is stored in Atlassian's cloud. Atlassian may process it outside the EU/EEA, for example in the United States, and protects such transfers with the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and its certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Retention periods are in the table in section 3. Export jobs and exported files expire by themselves.
  • Administrators can delete templates on the settings page of the App.
  • Personal data reporting: a scheduled function runs every day and reports the account ids that the App stores to Atlassian's personal data reporting service once every seven days. For an account that Atlassian reports as closed, the App erases that person's export jobs and files and removes the account id from the templates they uploaded.
  • Uninstalling the App removes it from the site; Atlassian removes the App's storage under its own retention rules.
  • Files that were attached to pages stay in Confluence, under the customer's control.

9. Security

The App follows the Forge security model. It requests granular scopes, plus one classic scope for attaching files to pages. It makes Confluence requests with the permissions of the user, and for exports that run after the user has left the dialog, as that same user through Atlassian's offline user impersonation. It checks its licence on the server and treats uploaded templates and page content as untrusted input: templates are opened by a careful reader that refuses macro-enabled files, old .doc files, password-protected files and zip bombs, tags are matched against a fixed list, and no code from a template is ever run. The interface runs under Forge's default content security policy without relaxations, and no page or template content is written to logs. Data in transit and at rest is protected by the Forge platform. We handle vulnerabilities within Atlassian's required time limits and notify Atlassian of security incidents within 48 hours.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to complain to a supervisory authority (in Sweden: Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY). For data in your Confluence site, please contact the administrator of that site first; for our own data contact support@karelialabs.com.

11. Children

The App is a business tool and is not directed at children.

12. Changes

We will update this policy when the App's data handling changes and publish the new version with a new date. Material changes are announced in the release notes.
