This draft follows the structure of the standard end user license terms that Atlassian offers Marketplace partners (a license to the customer for the app, subscription and fees via Atlassian, support, data, warranty and liability, term, governing law). Compare it with the current standard text in the Atlassian partner portal and prefer adopting that text unchanged where you can: a custom EULA needs a lawyer. Replace the bracketed placeholders. Nothing here is legal advice.

1. Definitions

  • Atlassian Product: the Atlassian Confluence Cloud site on which the App is installed.

  • Marketplace: the Atlassian Marketplace, through which the App is licensed and paid for.
  • Subscription: the Customer's license for the App, bought or trialled via the Marketplace.
  • Specification: an API description (OpenAPI, Swagger or AsyncAPI document) that a user attaches to a page or pastes into the App's settings.

2.2 The number of Authorised Users follows the user tier of the Subscription. A tier that the Marketplace lists as free is free of charge on the terms of that tier.

2.3 The App is licensed, not sold. The Provider keeps all rights in the App not expressly granted.

The Provider may change prices for future periods as the Marketplace allows.

5. Trial and evaluation

A trial license gives full functionality for the trial period shown in the Marketplace. After it ends the App stops showing Specifications on that site until a Subscription is bought.

6. Support and updates

6.1 The Provider provides support through the support page or by email at support@karelialabs.atlassian.net. The Provider aims to answer issues that Atlassian classifies as critical within 24 hours and other issues within 5 business days, during Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 Central European Time.

6.2 The Provider may release updates through the Marketplace. Updates may change or remove features; the Provider will not materially reduce the core functionality during a paid term without notice.

7. Customer data and privacy

7.1 The App runs on the Atlassian Forge platform ("Runs on Atlassian"). The Provider does not receive or have access to the Customer's content, and the App stores no data. How the App handles data is described in the Privacy Policy (https://ziroxonline111.github.io/karelia-labs/products/specfold/privacy/), which forms part of this Agreement.

7.2 The Customer is responsible for its content, including the Specifications it shows, for having the right to display them, and for who in its organisation may use the App. The App reads attachments with the permissions of the user who views the page; it cannot read more than that user can.

7.3 The App displays documentation only. It does not send requests to the APIs described in a Specification, and the examples and commands it shows are samples generated from the Specification.

7.4 Settings of the macro (including text that a user pastes) are stored by Confluence in the page. They are deleted with the page according to the Customer's Atlassian Product settings; the App holds no copy.

The Provider owns the App and its documentation. The Customer owns its content and Specifications. The Customer grants the Provider a free, perpetual license to use feedback and suggestions about the App without obligation.

The App uses the Atlassian Forge platform and open source components under their own licenses, which are listed with their license texts in the file THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt that ships with the App. The Atlassian Product and Forge are governed by the Customer's agreements with Atlassian.

The Provider gives no warranty of merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose, non-infringement, uninterrupted or error-free operation, or that every Specification is displayed completely or exactly as its author intended. The Customer should check the result against the Specification itself.

12.1 This Agreement runs while the App is installed or the Subscription is active.

12.2 Either party may terminate for the other's material breach that is not remedied within 30 days of notice. The Provider may suspend the license if the Subscription is not valid. On termination the Customer must stop using the App and uninstall it.

12.3 Sections 3, 8 and 10 to 14 survive termination.
