4. Fees, taxes and payment

Fees are set out on the Extension's Floxum page and are charged and collected through Floxum and its payment provider under Floxum's terms. Taxes, refunds, invoices and renewal are governed by Floxum's terms. The Provider may change prices for future periods as Floxum allows.

Fees charged by a Translation Service are separate. They are agreed between the Customer and that service and paid by the Customer. The Provider does not charge for translations and is not responsible for a Translation Service's fees, including fees caused by the Customer's settings or by use of the Customer's forum.

5. Trial

A trial gives full functionality for the trial period shown on Floxum. After it ends, a Subscription is needed to keep using the Extension.

6. Support and updates

6.1 The Provider provides support through the support page or by email at support@karelialabs.atlassian.net. The Provider aims to answer requests about errors that stop the Extension from working within 2 business days and other requests within 5 business days, during Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 Central European Time.

6.2 The Provider releases updates through Floxum and aims to support each new Flarum version within four weeks of its release. Updates may change or remove features; the Provider will not materially reduce the core functionality during a paid term without notice.

7. Customer data and privacy

7.1 The Extension runs on the Customer's own server. The Provider does not receive or have access to the Customer's forum data, posts or API keys. How the Extension handles personal data is described in the Privacy Policy (https://ziroxonline111.github.io/karelia-labs/products/karelia-translate/privacy/), which forms part of this Agreement.

7.2 When a reader asks for a translation, the Extension sends the text of the post to the Translation Service the Customer chose. The Customer is responsible for choosing that service, for having an agreement with it that covers this processing, for informing the forum's members where required, and for deciding which groups may translate.

7.3 The Customer can delete the Extension's stored data as described in the Privacy Policy.

8. Translations

Translations are produced by the Translation Service, not by the Provider. Machine translation can be inaccurate, incomplete or change the meaning of a text. Every translation is shown under the original with the name of the service that made it, and the original remains the authoritative text. The Customer is responsible for how translations are used on its Communities.

The Provider owns the Extension and its documentation. The Customer and its members own their content. The Customer grants the Provider a free, perpetual licence to use feedback and suggestions about the Extension without obligation.

10. Third-party components and services

The Extension runs on Flarum and uses open source components under their own licences, including the official Anthropic PHP SDK. Translation Services are provided by third parties under their own terms, and their availability, quality and prices are outside the Provider's control.

To the extent permitted by law the Extension is provided "as is" and "as available". The Provider gives no warranty of merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose, non-infringement, uninterrupted or error-free operation, or that translations are correct. The Customer should keep backups of its forum.

12.1 To the extent permitted by law the Provider is not liable for indirect, incidental, special or consequential damage, loss of profit, revenue, data or goodwill, or for charges from Translation Services.

12.2 The Provider's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees the Customer paid for the Extension in the twelve months before the event giving rise to the claim.

12.3 Nothing limits liability that cannot be limited by law, including for intent or gross negligence.

13.2 Either party may terminate for the other's material breach that is not remedied within 30 days of notice. On termination or when the Subscription ends, the Customer must stop using the Extension and remove it.
