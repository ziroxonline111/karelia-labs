Schools, universities, public bodies and companies can buy Captionwright Pro and Describewright Pro by invoice, without a card.

How it works

  1. Email hello@karelialabs.com with:
    • the product and the number of sites
    • your organisation's name and billing address
    • your purchase order number, if you use one
    • your VAT number, if you are in the EU
  2. You get a quote and an invoice within two working days.
  3. Pay by bank transfer within 30 days of the invoice date.
  4. Your licence key is emailed when the payment arrives. Public bodies and schools that send a signed purchase order get the key when the order arrives.

Papers we provide

  • An Accessibility Conformance Report (VPAT 2.5, WCAG edition) for each plugin: Captionwright and Describewright.
  • A W-8BEN form for buyers in the United States.
  • Answers to your security and privacy questionnaire.

Privacy in short

  • Captionwright: captions are made in the editor's own browser. Video, audio and captions stay on your WordPress site. Nothing is sent to Karelia Labs. Pro has an optional cloud mode that sends audio to the service you choose, with your own key.
  • Describewright: still frames and timing are sent to the AI service you choose, from your own server, only when an editor starts a description. Nothing is sent to Karelia Labs.

Try first

Captionwright has a free version on WordPress.org with no limit on videos or minutes. Pro licences are sold without refunds, so test the free version on your own site before you order.
